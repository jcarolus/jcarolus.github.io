MORE THAN THE NEXT MOVE

One app. A whole world of chess.

01

Find your kind of chess

Play standard chess, shuffle the starting position with Chess 960, or try experimental Duck chess.

02

Meet your next opponent

Connect to Lichess and FICS. Bring your game to a worldwide community of players.

03

Make your next move better

Explore the Lichess puzzle collection, solve mate-in-two challenges, and browse the ECO opening database.

04

A board on every screen

Play on your phone, tablet, or TV. With Chromecast support, give your game a bigger stage.

05

Your own chess workshop

Save and search games locally, set up positions in the board editor, and add third-party engines via OEX.

06

Small app. Open to everyone.

Just a few MB to install, with TalkBack screen reader support. Free, open source, no ads, no tracking.
